What am I looking at?
• Heart rate: how many times your heart beats per minute. Healthy resting range is 50–100 bpm. Elevations without exertion may signal stress, fever, or dehydration.
• Respiratory rate: breaths taken per minute (normal is 12–20 brpm). Rapid breathing can indicate anxiety or elevated cabin CO₂.
• Without a live vest sensor, use “Measure pulse” or “Measure breathing” and tap once per heartbeat or inhale.
